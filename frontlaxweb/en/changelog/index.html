What's new

What each version brings, newest first. The app updates itself from Settings, and the latest is 0.4.0.

Zooms you place, your own backgrounds and your webcam

Version 0.4.0 · 12 November 2026

  • Zooms you place: every zoom is a block on the timeline that you add, move, stretch or remove, and while recording Ctrl+Shift+9 drops one where the mouse is. You can also cut a piece out of the middle and speed up the slow parts.
  • Your own backgrounds (an image, your wallpaper, any colour or a video that moves with the recording) and your webcam, wherever you want it, in a circle or a square and with a border.
  • The pointer: smoothing none, little or a lot, a click sound and a ripple on every click, a motion trail, and it hides and zooms in while you type. With named styles, the same look carries over to the next recording.
  • Cover up personal data in a video with one click: emails, phone numbers, ID numbers, cards and accounts show up as mosaic blocks you can remove. And the mosaic is now coarse enough that the numbers cannot be guessed.
  • Screenshots: scrolling capture of a long page, a background with air and a shadow, a thumbnail in the corner that you can drag into any app, new marks, QR codes and a history of everything you captured.

Trim on the timeline

Version 0.3.8 · 8 November 2026

  • Trim on the timeline: drag its two blue edges and the preview shows the exact cut, down to the millisecond.
  • A new Studio panel: eight backgrounds, rounded corners and a shadow, a smooth pointer that loses the hand shake, the zoom speed and the volume.
  • A simpler editor: drawing tools on top, play and time next to the timeline, and the save buttons named.
  • Saving as GIF failed every time since 0.2.26: not any more.
  • Over a still screen, the pointer no longer freezes and then jumps in the saved video, and saving with a background is twice as fast.

Recordings keep their ending

Version 0.3.7 · 29 October 2026

  • Recordings no longer lose their ending when the screen stops changing: they last exactly what the recording bar said, pauses left out.
  • A still stretch in the middle of a recording keeps its full length, where before it was cut to 10 seconds.
  • Hovering highlights the window you can see, not a smaller one hidden behind it.
  • With several screens, the magnifier, the highlight and the mode bar only show on the one with the cursor, and the settings no longer say a shortcut is taken by another app when it was winshotx itself.
  • The Chinese translation is complete again, and thanks to Tyrone and wang13zhe, who found these bugs.

The toolbar no longer gets recorded

Version 0.3.6 · 21 October 2026

  • The recording toolbar no longer ends up inside the video, not even when recording the whole screen: you still see it and click it, and the video shows what is behind.
  • If your Windows does not support the modern capture, the settings say so, instead of leaving you guessing why photos of some windows come out with black patches.

Shots without black holes

Version 0.3.5 · 21 October 2026

  • Shots of windows drawn by the graphics card no longer come out with black holes.
  • Photos now use the same modern capture as recordings: in the test, 19 to 34 of every 80 photos used to come out wrong, and now none do.
  • Where Windows cannot use it without adding a yellow border, the old capture is still used, and taking a photo is no slower.

A clearer editor

Versions 0.3.3 to 0.3.4 · 17 October 2026

  • Recording a small strip, under 64 pixels a side, gives you a video again.
  • One-click aspect ratios: 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9, with the crop already placed and centred.
  • The editor opens right away when you rescue the last few seconds, and saving is 22% faster.
  • A clearer screen: save always in view, controls with names (Start and End instead of A and B) and settings that fit without scrolling.
  • The play button no longer waits forever if the notice saying the video is ready never arrives.

The marks follow the window

Version 0.3.2 · 16 October 2026

  • The click rings and the mouse trail no longer stay behind when you move the window you are recording: they go with it.

Record a single window

Version 0.3.1 · 16 October 2026

  • Press W with a window highlighted and only that window gets recorded.
  • Whatever passes in front of it (a notification, another window, the file explorer) no longer lands in the video.

Cover personal data in one click

Version 0.3.0 · 16 October 2026

  • The editor's shield looks for emails, phone numbers, cards, bank accounts and ID numbers, and drops a mosaic over each one.
  • It does not go by shape alone: it checks the check digit of the card and the IBAN, and the letter of the ID number, so it will not cover half a document by mistake.
  • Copy the text out of an image from inside the editor too, and from a recording.
  • Your latest shots live in the tray menu: a thumbnail and one click to put it back on the clipboard.
  • winshotx speaks Chinese, thanks to the translation wang13zhe sent in.

Capture straight into the editor

Version 0.2.28 · 15 October 2026

  • Win+Shift+L captures and opens the editor in one go, skipping the selection toolbar.
  • Handy when you already know you are going to point at something, cover up a detail or crop before sending it.
  • Your usual shortcut is untouched, and if another program has taken that combination, you can set your own in the settings.

Marks stay inside the image

Version 0.2.27 · 15 October 2026

  • The marks you draw on a shot (arrow, box, highlight, blur, numbered steps and text) no longer land outside the image.
  • If you crop the shot, whatever falls outside now disappears instead of showing up squashed against the border.
  • An arrow pointing outwards keeps its direction, and a step or a text against the edge fits whole.

Save wherever you want

Version 0.2.26 · 20 September 2026

  • Ctrl+Shift+S saves each shot wherever you want, with the folder and name you choose, and Ctrl+S still goes to your usual folder.
  • If you record while narrating with nothing playing on the speakers, your voice now makes it into the video.
  • The tray menu no longer sneaks into the video while you record.
  • It runs lighter: the last few seconds use four times less of your computer, and saving a video takes a third less time.

See what is being recorded

Version 0.2.25 · 12 September 2026

  • A thin red frame surrounds what is being recorded, and the bar says whether it goes to MP4 or GIF and whether sound is coming in.
  • The pointer in the video is your real cursor, with its own shape, and it can be drawn bigger without looking blocky or showing two.
  • The click zoom moves with inertia instead of jumping, and it shows live in the editor before you save.
  • Stopping a recording no longer makes you wait, because the editor opens right away, and pausing no longer pushes the sound out of sync.

The magnifier stays clear

Version 0.2.24 · 9 September 2026

  • The magnifier no longer sits on top of what you are looking at: near an edge it flips to the other side.
  • The action bar no longer ends up off screen: when the selection is as tall as the display, it moves inside, against the bottom edge.
  • With full screen and the action bar turned on, the bar now appears so you can copy, save, edit or pin, the same as with Ctrl+A.

The selection opens instantly

Version 0.2.23 · 5 September 2026

  • With three screens, the selection is already painted 114 milliseconds after the shortcut, against 590 before.
  • The “Getting the capture ready” screen is gone: no screen shows until its image is in place, and the one under the mouse goes first.

Much faster GIFs

Version 0.2.22 · 5 September 2026

  • Saving as GIF is 4 to 36 times faster depending on quality, with truer colours.
  • A GIF now lasts exactly as long as the recording did: it used to come out 10% too fast.
  • Cropping and copying answer sooner, because they no longer read the screen back from disk.

Options at hand while capturing

Versions 0.2.19 to 0.2.21 · 4 September 2026

  • Whether the action bar appears when you let go of the mouse is now chosen on the top bar (or with B), and it is remembered for next time.
  • The last few seconds step aside for half a second while a capture opens, so the shortcut is not slowed down, and the rescued clip has no gap in it.
  • The Smoothness row says what the measured combinations cost, and the most expensive one says it in amber.
  • Start with Windows launches the installed copy, not an old one that asked to update every time you turned the computer on.
  • If your Windows lacks the component winshotx draws its windows with, it now says so in a dialog instead of opening an error page.

Captures open sooner

Versions 0.2.13 to 0.2.18 · 31 August 2026

  • With several screens, they are all frozen at once instead of one after another, so the selection shows up sooner and more steadily.
  • Back to back captures open sooner and no longer get slower one after another.
  • The first capture of the day opens sooner too, because the selection windows get ready while the app starts.
  • 0.2.13 could freeze right after installing: fixed in 0.2.14.

Leave with a single Esc

Version 0.2.12 · 31 August 2026

  • A single Esc closes the capture, where the first one used to just clear the selection.

Saving videos 4.5 times faster

  • Copying a video leaves the file and its path too: where files can be dropped you paste the video, and in a text box, the path.

Copy takes what you drew

The last few seconds, always recorded

Update with one button

Versions 0.1.2 to 0.1.3 · 21 August 2026

  • Every download is checked against a signature, so a tampered installer is rejected.
  • Anyone on 0.1.1 or older has to install this one by hand, because those versions cannot tell them.
  • The magnifier no longer picks the wrong pixel near the screen edges, and the colour no longer freezes while dragging.

Version 0.1.1 · 21 August 2026

  • Ctrl+S saves from the editor without reaching for the mouse.
  • The recording bar warns in amber when the cache goes over 1 GB.
  • A still shot no longer opens as MP4 instead of PNG, and two clicks on Save no longer start two exports.
  • Running winshotx twice no longer leaves the shortcuts fighting, and emptying the cache with the editor open no longer pulls its frames away.

First public release

Version 0.1.0 · 20 August 2026

  • Capture a region over the frozen screen, with a magnifier that gives the exact colour of each pixel, or a whole window with one click.
  • Record the region at 15, 30 or 60 frames per second.
  • An editor with thumbnails, start and end trimming, loop playback and resizing that keeps the proportions.
  • Save as GIF, MP4 or PNG, to disk or to the clipboard, with shortcuts you set by pressing the keys.
  • A 1.5 MB installer, no administrator rights and nothing else to install.
