Capture down to the pixel

The screen freezes when you press your shortcut and you crop over a still picture, so nothing moves while you choose.

  • A 6× magnifier with the exact colour
  • One click takes the whole window
  • A long page, whole, scrolling by itself

Record what happens

MP4 or GIF of an area, a window or the whole screen, with your voice and your webcam if you want.

  • The PC sound and your microphone, each on its own track
  • Your webcam, in a circle or with the corners you like
  • One key keeps the last 30 seconds

Get it ready to show

The editor trims on the timeline and adds a background, corners and a shadow without leaving the window.

  • Zooms you place, or on every click
  • Cut a piece out of the middle and speed up the slow bits
  • Hides emails, phone numbers and cards in one click

Your AI agent can record the screen too

Claude Code, Codex or Cursor record, stop, export the video and take screenshots with winshotx, with one command or through its MCP server. While they record you see the frame, as always.

Your agent records the demo. You go grab a coffee.

How to connect it
PowerShell
# once: add it to Claude Code
PS> claude mcp add --scope user winshotx -- "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\winshotx\winshotx.exe" mcp
# or from any terminal
PS> winshotx agent record --screen 0 --max-seconds 20
PS> winshotx agent stop
{"ok":true,"session":"3f9c0a1b2d", ...}
PS> winshotx agent export 3f9c0a1b2d --out demo.mp4 --zoom-on-clicks 0.5

Ask on GitHub

Is winshotx free?

Yes, all of it. It's open source under the MIT licence: the full code is on GitHub, with no time limit and no feature locked behind a payment.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

No. It never asks for an account, sends nothing to any cloud, and works offline; the only moment it talks to the internet is to check for a new version.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 (version 1903 or later), Windows 11 and, since version 0.6.0, Linux, with X11 or with GNOME on Wayland: a .deb for Debian and Ubuntu, an .rpm for Fedora and an AppImage for the rest, which does not save MP4 video. Not on macOS yet.

Which languages is the app in?

Spanish, English and Chinese. It follows your Windows language on its own, and in Settings you can pick another one whenever you like. Every label, every explanation and the welcome screen are translated.

How do you install and update it?

The installer is 3.03 MB and goes to your user, not the system, so Windows never asks for administrator rights. It updates itself from inside the app with one button, and every download is signed and checked before anything is installed.

Can it be installed with winget?

Yes. It is published in the official winget-pkgs catalogue, so winget install Mun1to.winshotx installs it and winget upgrade Mun1to.winshotx updates it. A new version takes a few hours to show up in the catalogue: while it does, the app updates itself from Settings.

Is it on the Microsoft Store?

Yes, since 7 September 2026: apps.microsoft.com. It is the same app, and there the package is signed by Microsoft and the Store keeps it updated, so the app's own updater stays out of the way. The installer from this page and winget give you exactly the same thing.

What can it capture and record?

An area, a window or the whole screen, and a long page scrolling by itself; video and GIF at 15, 30 or 60 fps with the PC sound and your microphone on separate tracks, and your webcam if you want; the last 15, 30 or 60 seconds without having pressed record; and an editor with zooms you place or on every click, your own backgrounds, corners and shadow, trimming on the timeline, ten marks to annotate, a mosaic, and personal data covered up in one click.

Can AI agents use it?

Yes. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any agent that uses the terminal records, stops, exports and captures with the winshotx agent command, and the ones that speak MCP get it as a server with winshotx mcp. While they record you see the frame, as always.

What does it still lack compared to the Windows Snipping Tool?

Two things. It does not come preinstalled: you have to install it. And it speaks three languages, Spanish, English and Chinese, while the Windows one speaks all of them. The point by point comparison is just below, including where winshotx loses.
