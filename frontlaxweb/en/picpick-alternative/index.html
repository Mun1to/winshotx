PicPick alternative for Windows

winshotx is a PicPick alternative for Windows 10 and 11 and for Linux, free and with no account. Its installer weighs 3.01 MB; PicPick's, 69 MB. Where winshotx wins: free, for work too; a video with zooms that follow your clicks, and a background; and covers the personal data in a video by itself. Where PicPick wins: speaks more languages and uploads the shot and gives you a link.

How to get started

  1. Install winshotx. It weighs 3.01 MB, installs for your user only and never shows the administrator prompt.
  2. Capture with Ctrl+Shift+2. Drag over what you want.
  3. Record with Ctrl+Shift+5. Drag over the area, and press the same shortcut again to stop.
  4. Change the shortcuts if you like. In Settings, click the field and press the combination you want.

Step by step: record your screen, take a screenshot and record a GIF.
