ScreenToGif alternative for Windows

winshotx is a ScreenToGif alternative for Windows 10 and 11 and for Linux, free and with no account. Its installer weighs 3.03 MB; ScreenToGif's, 75 MB. Where winshotx wins: a video with zooms that follow your clicks, and a background; covers the personal data in a video by itself; and an AI agent can drive it. Where ScreenToGif wins: speaks more languages; uploads the shot and gives you a link; and edits a GIF frame by frame.

How to get started

  1. Install winshotx. It weighs 3.03 MB, installs for your user only and never shows the administrator prompt.
  2. Capture with Ctrl+Shift+2. Drag over what you want.
  3. Record with Ctrl+Shift+5. Drag over the area, and press the same shortcut again to stop.
  4. Change the shortcuts if you like. In Settings, click the field and press the combination you want.
