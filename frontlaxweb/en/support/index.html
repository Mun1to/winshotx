Support

Something not working as it should? Here is how to get help and report a bug, the questions that always come up, where winshotx keeps your things, how to remove it completely and, if it saves you time, how to buy me a coffee.

Get help or report a bug

Everything goes through the GitHub issues, which are public: that way anyone with the same problem finds the answer. Check first whether it is already there; if not, open a new one. From the app you get to the same place: Settings → Help and winshotx → Report a bug.

Report a bug See the ones already reported

So it can be fixed the first time, include:

  • The winshotx version. It is at the top of Settings, next to the name.
  • Your Windows: 10 or 11, and its version (Win + R, type winver and press Enter).
  • How you installed it: from this website, with winget, from the Microsoft Store or the portable build.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened, step by step, so it can be repeated.
  • Your screens, if it goes wrong when capturing or recording: how many you have and the scale of each one (Settings → System → Display).
  • A screenshot or a short video, if it can be seen. Cover anything personal first: winshotx does it by itself, with one click.

If you have no GitHub account, or it is something that should not be public (a security problem, for example), write to winshotx@gmail.com.

The questions that always come up

I installed it and I see nothing

It lives in the system tray, with no window of its own. Windows hides new icons: if you cannot see it, it is behind the ^ arrow on the taskbar. To keep it in sight, drag it from there onto the taskbar.

Print Screen still opens the Snipping Tool

In Settings → Windows keys there is a switch that takes the key from the Snipping Tool and gives it to winshotx. Win + Shift + S has a switch of its own, with a price it tells you about first: it takes Win + S, the search, with it. The details, in the docs.

The video has no microphone or no webcam

First check that Windows lets apps use them: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone (or Camera), with Let desktop apps access your microphone turned on (in Windows 10, under Privacy). Then, that they are turned on in winshotx before recording: it only records what you turn on.

How do I update it?

It updates itself. The one from this website and the one from winget ask GitHub whether there is a new version and install it with one click from Settings, checking its signature first; the one from the Microsoft Store is updated by the Store. What each version brings, in what's new.

Not here? The rest of the questions are in the docs and on the home page.

Where it keeps your things

Nothing leaves your computer (the privacy page explains it). This is what it writes, and where:

  • Your screenshots and videos, in the folder you choose in Settings → The app. By default, Pictures\winshotx.
  • Your settings, in %APPDATA%\com.munir.winshotx: the settings.json file, and the backgrounds you add.
  • The text read from your screenshots, so you can search them in History, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.munir.winshotx.
  • The temporary files: the recording you are editing, what always recording the last seconds keeps, and the pinned screenshots, in %TEMP%\winshotx. It is cleaned up every time winshotx starts, and Settings → The app empties the recording cache with one button.

To open one of those folders, paste its name, % signs included, into the File Explorer address bar. In the Microsoft Store version, Windows keeps the settings and the text inside the app's package, and deletes them when you uninstall it.

How to uninstall it

First, if you gave winshotx a Windows key (Print Screen or Win + Shift + S), turn its switch off in Settings → Windows keys: that puts them back exactly as they were, and the uninstaller does not.

  • From this website or with winget: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → winshotx → Uninstall (in Windows 10, Apps and features). The uninstaller has a box, Delete the application data: tick it and it also removes your settings and the text of your screenshots.
  • From the Microsoft Store: right-click winshotx in the Start menu → Uninstall. Windows removes its settings with it.
  • The portable build: quit it from the tray and delete its folder. Its settings are in the folders above, and they go by hand.

With winget, from the terminal too:

winget uninstall Mun1to.winshotx

No uninstaller touches your screenshots and videos: they are yours, and they stay in their folder.
